## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a dignified, contemporary, and deeply respectful digital presence for community-level Islamic organizations within the Nahdlatul Ulama tradition. The aesthetic blends institutional clarity, civic warmth, and modern Islamic sobriety. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Ta'awun (Mutual Cooperation) & Trust:** The visual tone is grounded, transparent, and service-oriented. It inspires trust across multiple generations—from senior *Kyai* and *Mustasyar* to tech-forward youth in autonomous bodies (*Banom*).
- **Muted Dignity:** Avoid decorative orientalism, faux-gilded textures, or excessive arabesque patterns. Instead, express Islamic identity through structured proportions, generous whitespace, pristine typographic hierarchy, and deliberate use of deep emerald and warm goldenrod.
- **Civic Accessibility:** The system prioritizes legibility, intuitive CMS operation, and fast performance across varying connection speeds and mobile devices in rural-suburban community environments.

### Design Movement
**Modern Institutional Minimalism with Tactile Warmth:**
- Pure architectural surfaces balanced by subtle warm gray tints (`#F8F9FA` to `#F4F6F4`).
- Precision grid discipline paired with delicate, high-contrast structural borders.
- Vibrant, meaningful badging systems to differentiate the autonomous wings (Ansor, Banser, Fatayat, IPNU, IPPNU) while preserving central structural harmony.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the venerable green of Nahdlatul Ulama, supported by warm amber accents reflecting barakah and honor, with clean institutional neutrals ensuring optimal contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0C5A37` - Emerald Verdant):** The core institutional identity. Used for primary navigation, critical call-to-actions, institutional headers, and authoritative state indicators. 
  - *Light tints (`#E8F5E9`, `#D1E7DD`)* serve as soft background tints for prayer time cards, verified tags, and official decrees (*Surat Edaran*).
  - *Deep shades (`#083D25`)* anchor active pressed states and high-contrast footers.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Qindil Gold):** Symbolic of illumination, intellectual heritage, and service. Reserved strictly for highlights, active prayer indicators, celebratory badges, donation milestones (Infaq/Sedekah), and contextual accents.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A8A` - Civic Indigo):** Supporting structural tone used for public administration records, administrative CMS labels, and cross-wing archival tags.
- **Neutral Palette (`#111827` to `#FFFFFF`):**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` for content cards and articles; `#F9FAF8` (warm tinted white) for outer page canvas.
  - **Surface Borders:** `#E5E7EB` (subtle border) and `#D1D5DB` (active focus/outline).
  - **Text High-Emphasis:** `#111827` (almost black, optimal WCAG AAA readability).
  - **Text Medium-Emphasis:** `#4B5563` (for metadata, timestamps, and hijri dates).
  - **Text Muted:** `#6B7280` (for disclaimers and administrative hints).

### Autonomous Body (Banom) Specific Semantic Badges
To respect brand identities within the NU ecosystem without diluting the primary UI:
- **GP Ansor:** Deep Forest Green (`#0A5C36`) & Gold (`#B45309`)
- **Banser:** Cordovan Camo Olive (`#3F4F34`) & Signal Red (`#DC2626`)
- **Fatayat NU:** Fresh Jade Green (`#059669`) & Soft Emerald (`#ECFDF5`)
- **Muslimat NU:** Classical Olive Green (`#15803D`)
- **IPNU:** Royal Sky Blue (`#0284C7`) & Emerald Green
- **IPPNU:** Bright Sage Green (`#10B981`) & Lemon Ochre (`#CA8A04`)

## Typography

The type system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing crisp geometric authority balanced by humanistic warmth that performs exceptionally across bilingual contexts (Indonesian and standard Indonesian Islamic transliteration / pegon romanization).

### Typographic Principles
- **Clarity Over Grandeur:** Noble headings avoid archaic serifs, using tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and firm font weights (`700`, `800`) to evoke civic stability and modern relevance.
- **Content-First Editorial Reading:** News articles, tausiyah, and official rulings (*Bahtsul Masail*) rely on `body-md` and `body-lg` set with generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.65`) for prolonged reading comfort across village elders and students alike.
- **Metadata Disciplines:** Hijri calendars, prayer timestamps, and Banom markers use uppercase or tracked `label-md` and `label-sm` weights to prevent visual competition with narrative headings.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid with an adaptive maximum container width of `1280px` for public portal views, and a flexible fluid workspace layout for the internal CMS console.

### Breakpoints & Container Dynamics
- **Mobile (0 - 639px):** 4-column layout, `16px` (`margin-mobile`) canvas padding, `16px` gutters. Components reflow to a single column; horizontal scrolling chips manage quick filters for Banom tags and prayer times.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `24px` margins, `20px` gutters. Dashboard and publication sections collapse into 2-column card structures.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, `32px` to `48px` margins, `24px` (`gutter`) space. The layout supports asymmetrical compositions, such as a 8-column main content stream for Tausiyah/News paired with a 4-column sidebar for Prayer Schedules, Sholawat agendas, and Lazisnu donation meters.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`):** Reserved for button internals, form field padding, badge spacing, and list item gaps.
- **Macro Spacing (`space-lg` to `space-3xl`):** Governs card paddings, section headers, modular CMS forms, and footer separation, preventing visual crowding and maintaining a serene, organized presence.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic, accessible tone, this system avoids heavy skeuomorphism and muddy dark drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering combined with subtle ambient light occlusion**.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#F9FAF8` (gentle warm alabaster), establishing an unpretentious paper-like base.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards, Widgets):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` resting directly on Level 0, bounded by a 1px border of `#E5E7EB`. A whisper ambient shadow (`0px 1px 3px rgba(12, 90, 55, 0.04)`) anchors the surface without drawing attention to itself.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Flyouts):** Elevated with a subtle green-tinted atmospheric shadow (`0px 8px 20px -4px rgba(12, 90, 55, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`), accompanied by a border transition to `#D1E7DD`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Confirmation Sheets, Drawer Menus):** High floating layer with soft scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.45)`) and distinct elevation (`0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(12, 90, 55, 0.12), 0px 10px 10px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`).

### Outline & Structural Line System
- High-contrast ghost borders (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) are used extensively around input containers, agenda item rows, and CMS data tables to communicate deliberate organization and administrative precision.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry. This strikes an intentional midpoint: neither cold and razor-sharp (which feels overly bureaucratic) nor overly bubble-shaped (which diminishes formal religious and civic gravitas).

### Corner Geometry Specifications
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Standard buttons, text inputs, dropdown triggers, compact alert strips, table cells.
- **Containers & Surfaces (`1rem` / `16px`):** Editorial cards, prayer time highlight boards, CMS modules, public modal dialogs.
- **Sub-structures (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Checkbox boxes, inner input controls, segmented control tabs.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Exclusively designated for status badges, Banom indicator tags, category pills, and avatar rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald background (`#0C5A37`), pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), `8px` border radius, semi-bold typography (`label-lg`). Hover state deepens to `#083D25`. Focused with a 3px outer ring tinted at 30% primary green (`rgba(12, 90, 55, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Accent:** Warm Amber fill (`#D97706`), white text, used for high-urgency civic actions (e.g., *Bayar ZISWAF*, *Konfirmasi Kehadiran Istighotsah*).
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent surface with `1px solid #D1D5DB`, neutral dark text (`#1F2937`). On hover, acquires surface `#F4F6F4` and border `#0C5A37`.
- **Destructive (CMS):** Crimson soft wash (`#FEE2E2`) with `#B91C1C` text and border for CMS archiving or unpublishing.

### Badges & Banom Chips
- Compact, pill-shaped (`9999px`) metadata markers with `label-sm` or `label-md` weight.
- Composed with low-opacity tinted backgrounds (`10% - 15%`) and darkened foreground text to ensure strict AA accessibility.
- Banom chips (e.g., `Ansor`, `Fatayat`, `IPNU`, `IPPNU`, `Banser`) feature an optional `6px` solid status dot preceding the autonomous body acronym.

### Input Fields & Form Controls (CMS & Public)
- Height: `44px` (desktop), `48px` (touch mobile).
- Border: `1px solid #D1D5DB` on white canvas; transitions to `1.5px solid #0C5A37` with an ambient glow on `:focus`.
- Labels are positioned outside the field in `label-lg`, high-emphasis `#1F2937`.
- Helper text and error feedback sit cleanly below the container using `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- `18px x 18px` touch target with `24px` interactive touch padding.
- Checkbox uses `4px` corner rounding; radio is fully circular.
- Selected state fills with `#0C5A37` carrying an interior crisp white geometric tick or dot.

### Cards
- **Article / Berita Ranting Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5E7EB`, `16px` radius. Features a media aspect ratio of 16:9, a Banom badge anchored at the top-left of the content stack, an authoritative `headline-sm` title, and an author attribution block with Hijri & Gregorian dual dates.
- **Jadwal Sholat & Imsakiyah Widget:** Emerald gradient header accent stripe (`3px` top border), subtle tabular arrangement showing each prayer time, with the current/upcoming prayer time highlighted in soft amber (`#FEF3C7` surface, `#92400E` text).
- **Lazisnu Infaq Meter:** Includes an accessible structured progress bar (`height: 8px`, rounded), explicit target vs. realized numerical labels, and direct CTA integration.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Border-separated or surface-alternated rows for agendas, Bahtsul Masail archives, and death notices (*Lelayu/Kabar Duka*).
- Pinned announcements feature a left accent border (`3px solid #0C5A37`).
- Hover states on list items trigger a subtle wash of `#F4F6F4` without shifting layout elements.
